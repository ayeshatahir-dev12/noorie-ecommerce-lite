About Noorié

Noorié is more than a candle — it’s a sensory ritual. Born from a passion for slow living and intentional design, each piece is hand-poured using clean, sustainable ingredients and wrapped in timeless elegance.

We believe that scent holds memory, and with every flicker of flame, we invite you to soften, slow down, and reconnect. From earthy spice to floral calm, our fragrances are crafted to transform ordinary spaces into intimate sanctuaries.

Welcome to Noorié — where light meets soul.
